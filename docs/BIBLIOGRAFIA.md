# Bibliografía y fuentes ICIV

Referencias que sostienen el diseño metodológico, el marco teórico de la tesis y el catálogo de datos. Formato APA (7.ª ed.) abreviado. Antes de la entrega final, confirmar páginas y DOI de cada referencia en su fuente original.

---

## 1. Indicadores compuestos: construcción, ponderación y robustez

1. **OECD & JRC. (2008).** *Handbook on constructing composite indicators: Methodology and user guide*. OECD Publishing. — Estándar de referencia para todo el diseño del ICIV.
2. **Nardo, M., Saisana, M., Saltelli, A., Tarantola, S., Hoffman, A., & Giovannini, E. (2005).** *Handbook on constructing composite indicators: Methodology and user guide* (OECD Statistics Working Papers 2005/03). OECD Publishing.
3. **Saaty, T. L. (1980).** *The analytic hierarchy process: Planning, priority setting, resource allocation*. McGraw-Hill. — Base del AHP y de la razón de consistencia.
4. **Saaty, T. L. (1990).** How to make a decision: The analytic hierarchy process. *European Journal of Operational Research, 48*(1), 9–26.
5. **Saisana, M., Saltelli, A., & Tarantola, S. (2005).** Uncertainty and sensitivity analysis techniques as tools for the quality assessment of composite indicators. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series A, 168*(2), 307–323. — Fundamento del análisis de sensibilidad.
6. **Saltelli, A. (2007).** Composite indicators between analysis and advocacy. *Social Indicators Research, 81*(1), 65–77.
7. **Paruolo, P., Saisana, M., & Saltelli, A. (2013).** Ratings and rankings: Voodoo or science? *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series A, 176*(3), 609–634.
8. **Becker, W., Saisana, M., Paruolo, P., & Vandecasteele, I. (2017).** Weights and importance in composite indicators: Closing the gap. *Ecological Indicators, 80*, 12–22.
9. **Greco, S., Ishizaka, A., Tasiou, M., & Torrisi, G. (2019).** On the methodological framework of composite indices: A review of the issues of weighting, aggregation, and robustness. *Social Indicators Research, 141*(1), 61–94.

## 2. Clima de inversión, instituciones e inversión extranjera

11. **Dunning, J. H. (1980).** Toward an eclectic theory of international production: Some empirical tests. *Journal of International Business Studies, 11*(1), 9–31.
12. **Bekaert, G., & Harvey, C. R. (2003).** Emerging markets finance. *Journal of Empirical Finance, 10*(1–2), 3–55.
13. **Bénassy-Quéré, A., Coupet, M., & Mayer, T. (2007).** Institutional determinants of foreign direct investment. *The World Economy, 30*(5), 764–782.
14. **Busse, M., & Hefeker, C. (2007).** Political risk, institutions and foreign direct investment. *European Journal of Political Economy, 23*(2), 397–415.
15. **Kaufmann, D., Kraay, A., & Mastruzzi, M. (2010).** *The Worldwide Governance Indicators: Methodology and analytical issues* (Policy Research Working Paper 5430). World Bank.
16. **World Bank. (2024).** *Business Ready 2024*. World Bank. — Sucesor del informe *Doing Business*, descontinuado en 2021.
